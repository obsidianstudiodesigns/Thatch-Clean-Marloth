Privacy policy

Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA). Last updated November 2026.

Thatch Clean Marloth ("Thatch Clean", "we", "us") cleans, fumigates and restores thatch and timber in Marloth Park. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you contact us or ask for a quote, why we need it, and the rights you have under POPIA.

1. Who is responsible

The responsible party is Thatch Clean, owned and run by Andre Wiid, Marloth Park, Mpumalanga. Andre is also our Information Officer. You can reach him on 082 563 1277 (call or WhatsApp) or at wiidandre59@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

  • Your name, phone or WhatsApp number and email address.
  • The address or area of the property, and details about the work you need (for example the size of the thatch area, photos you send us and when it was last cleaned).
  • Records of quotes, bookings, invoices and payments.

This website does not take payments and does not ask for card or banking details.

3. How the quote form works

The quote form on this website does not store anything on a server. When you press "Send on WhatsApp", your browser opens WhatsApp with your message filled in, and nothing is sent until you send it yourself. Messages you send us on WhatsApp are also handled under WhatsApp's own privacy terms.

4. Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, visit the property and give you a quote.
  • To schedule and carry out the work, and to follow up afterwards.
  • To keep the accounting and tax records the law requires.

We only use your information for these purposes. We do not sell or rent it, and we will not send you marketing messages without your consent.

5. Who we share it with

We share your information only when we need to: with team members working on your job, with service providers we rely on to run the business (such as our phone, email and accounting providers), or when the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to work are deleted within 12 months. Quotes, invoices and job records are kept for as long as tax law requires (currently five years), then deleted.

7. Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to protect your information against loss, misuse and unauthorised access, including password-protected devices and accounts.

8. This website

This website does not use advertising cookies or tracking analytics, and it does not ask you to log in. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may keep basic technical logs (such as IP addresses) to keep the service secure. Links to WhatsApp, Facebook and email open services that have their own privacy policies.

9. Your rights

Under POPIA you may ask what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct or delete it, object to how we use it, and withdraw consent you have given. Contact Andre using the details above. If you are not happy with our response you may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.

10. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page.
